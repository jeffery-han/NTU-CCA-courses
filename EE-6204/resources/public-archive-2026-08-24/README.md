# EE-6204 public resource archive (2026-08-24)

This archive contains public, course-specific material found during an Exa-assisted search on 2026-08-24. It is retained as study material, not as an official NTU distribution or authoritative marking scheme.

## Sources and contents

- [freshman2233/EE6204-System-Analysis](https://github.com/freshman2233/EE6204-System-Analysis) (Apache-2.0 repository): 29 question-level personal walkthroughs from 2015, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023; 59 worked slide examples; 11 PDF explainers (including an appendix); one topic-analysis workbook; and an abridged course outline.
- [NTU-CCA/EE6204](https://github.com/NTU-CCA/EE6204): six publicly posted past-exam solution compilations for AY2012-13, AY2013-14, AY2014-15, AY2015-16, AY2017-18, and AY2018-19.
- [chesha1/NTU_SP_Courses](https://github.com/chesha1/NTU_SP_Courses) was used only as contextual cross-checking. Its course comments are not treated as official policy and no textbook links were imported.

The source repositories' README and licence files are retained under `source-metadata/` where available.

## Archive layout

- `past-exam-solutions-2012-2019/`: multi-question solution compilations; provenance does not establish that they are official solutions.
- `past-exam-walkthroughs-2015-2023/`: personal question-by-question walkthroughs.
- `worked-slide-examples/`: worked examples grouped by the source repository's LP/NLP, stochastic-process/queueing, and decision-analysis structure.
- `topic-explainers/`: supplementary student explainers and one topic-analysis workbook.
- `course-outline/`: abridged OBTL/course-outline PDF.
