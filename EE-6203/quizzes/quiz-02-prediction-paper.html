45:00

Question (25 marks). Conventions follow the LPH lecture notes: \(\mathbf x(k+1)=\mathbf{\Phi}(T)\mathbf x(k)+\mathbf{\Theta}(T)u(k)\), state feedback \(u(k)=-\mathbf K\mathbf x(k)\).

(a) · 8 marks

A continuous-time plant is

\[\dot{\mathbf x}(t)=\begin{bmatrix}-1&1\\0&-2\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t)+\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}u(t),\qquad y(t)=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t).\]

It is driven through a zero-order hold and sampled with period \(T=\ln 2\) s.

(i) Find \(\mathbf{\Phi}(T)\) and \(\mathbf{\Theta}(T)\) of the discretised state-space model.
(ii) Where are the poles of the discrete-time system located?

(b) · 5 marks

Find the pulse transfer function \(Y(z)/U(z)\) of the discretised model in (a), and confirm that its poles agree with your answer to (a)(ii).

(c) · 6 marks

For the discretised model in (a):
(i) show that it is controllable;
(ii) design the deadbeat state-feedback gain \(\mathbf K\) using Ackermann's formula;
(iii) verify your design by computing the state sequence from \(\mathbf x(0)=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\end{bmatrix}^T\).

(d) · 6 marks

A discrete-time process has transfer function

\[G(z)=\frac{Y(z)}{U(z)}=\frac{z-0.5}{z^2-0.3z-0.1}.\]

(i) Write down its controllable canonical form (CCF) state-space model by inspection.
(ii) Test the controllability and the observability of this realisation.
(iii) Explain your result in (ii) in terms of \(G(z)\).
