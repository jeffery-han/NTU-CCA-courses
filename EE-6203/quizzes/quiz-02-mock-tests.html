Question 1 · 8 marks

(i) A voltage source \(e(t)\) drives a series loop of a resistor \(R=4\ \Omega\), an inductor \(L=1\) H and a capacitor \(C=\tfrac13\) F. The output is the capacitor voltage, \(y=v_C\). Using the states \(x_1=v_C\) and \(x_2=i\) (the loop current), derive the state-space model \(\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf A\mathbf x+\mathbf Be,\ y=\mathbf C\mathbf x\). (3)

(ii) Find a state-space model of the system below, with the states labelled at the block outputs. (3)

(iii) Find \(Y(s)/E(s)\) for (i) and \(Y(s)/U(s)\) for (ii). Comment on what you find. (2)

Question 2 · 10 marks

A continuous-time plant

\[\dot{\mathbf x}(t)=\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\1&-2\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t)+\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}u(t),\qquad y(t)=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t)\]

is preceded by a zero-order hold and sampled with \(T=0.5\) s.

(i) Find the discrete-time model \(\mathbf x(k+1)=\mathbf\Phi(T)\mathbf x(k)+\mathbf\Theta(T)u(k)\). (5)
(ii) Is the discrete-time model controllable? Justify. (2)
(iii) Find the pulse transfer function \(Y(z)/U(z)\) by formula, and state its poles. (3)

Question 3 · 7 marks
\[\mathbf x(k+1)=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0.5\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(k)+\begin{bmatrix}0.5\\1\end{bmatrix}u(k)\]

(i) Design the state-feedback gain \(\mathbf K\), with \(u(k)=-\mathbf K\mathbf x(k)\), that gives a deadbeat response. (5)
(ii) With \(\mathbf x(0)=[1\ \ 1]^T\), compute \(\mathbf x(1)\) and \(\mathbf x(2)\) and show that the response is deadbeat. (2)

Question 2 · 10 marks
\[\dot{\mathbf x}(t)=\begin{bmatrix}1&\beta\\0&2\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t)+\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}u(t),\qquad y(t)=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\end{bmatrix}\mathbf x(t),\qquad T=0.5\ \text{s (ZOH)}\]

(i) Find the discrete-time state-space model, in terms of \(\beta\). (5)
(ii) Find \(\mathbf X(z)/U(z)\) in terms of \(\beta\). (3)
