Question 1 10 marks

A unity-feedback digital loop (sampler, ZOH, plant) has open-loop pulse transfer function \[G(z)=\frac{K(0.4261z+0.3608)}{(z-1)(z-0.6065)},\qquad T=1\ \text{s}.\]

7

(a) Find the closed-loop characteristic equation and the range of \(K\) for stability.

3

(b) Find \(K_v\) as a function of \(K\), and the steady-state error to a unit ramp when \(K=0.5\).

Question 2 10 marks

A continuous system \(G(s)=\dfrac{4}{s^2+2s+4}\) is to be approximated digitally with \(T=0.5\) s.

4

(a) Find \(G(z)\) by the bilinear transformation. Check the DC gain and whether \(G(z)\) is stable.

3

(b) Find the strictly proper pole-zero matched \(G(z)\), with the DC gain matched.

3

(c) Write the difference equation that implements your answer to (a) by direct programming. How many delay elements does it use, and how many would standard programming use?

Question 3 10 marks

Unity feedback with a digital controller \(C(z)\) in front of \(G_{ZAS}(z)\). The desired closed loop is \(G_{cl}(z)=z^{-1}\).

5

(a) \(G_{ZAS}(z)=\dfrac{0.2(z+0.6)}{(z-0.8)(z-0.4)}\). Can \(G_{cl}=z^{-1}\) be used, and will the output track a step? If so, find \(C(z)\).

5

(b) Repeat for \(G_{ZAS}(z)=\dfrac{0.1(z+1.5)}{(z-1)(z-0.5)}\). If \(z^{-1}\) is unacceptable, explain why, choose a valid \(G_{cl}\), and find \(C(z)\) and the output \(y(k)\) for \(k=0,\dots,3\).
