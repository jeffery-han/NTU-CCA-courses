How to use this

  • This is a measurement, not practice. Sit it closed-book, calculator allowed, transform table allowed — the same conditions as the quiz. Guessing inflates the score and hides the gap.
  • Untimed. Budget roughly 90 minutes. Per-topic scores mean nothing if you rush.
  • Full worked solutions are deliberately not in this file. They are in quiz-01-evaluation-set-solutions.pdf. Marking here tells you which answers were wrong and names the likely mistake; the derivation is in the PDF.
  • Read the bars, not the total. A topic under 60 % is where the remaining study hours belong. Every question carries the lecture pages that cover it.
  • Numeric answers are marked with a tolerance. Enter a plain decimal number.
