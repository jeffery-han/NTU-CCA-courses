Q1. Classifier evaluation (40 marks)

A screening model is tested on 400 patients: 160 have the disease (positive) and 240 do not. The model misses 25 of the diseased patients and wrongly flags 30 of the healthy ones.

  1. Construct the confusion matrix, with rows = predicted class and columns = actual class. Label TP, FP, FN and TN. (8)
  2. Compute the accuracy, error rate, sensitivity (recall), specificity, precision and F-score. Show the formula and substitution for each. (24)
  3. The hospital then tests on a population with twice as many healthy patients (480). The model behaves the same within each class. Which of your six metrics stay the same and which change? Give the new precision and F-score, and explain the difference in one sentence. (8)

Q2. k-means clustering (40 marks)

Cluster the seven points below into k = 2 groups with k-means, using Euclidean distance. The initial centroids are \(c_1 = A\) and \(c_2 = B\).

PointABCDEFG
\((x, y)\)(1, 1)(2, 1)(1, 3)(5, 4)(6, 5)(4, 5)(3, 3)
  1. Iteration 1: compute each point's distance to both centroids (squared distances are fine), assign the points, and compute the new centroids. (14)
  2. Iteration 2: reassign the points and update the centroids. Say which point changes cluster and why. (14)
  3. Show that the algorithm has converged and state the final clusters. (6)
  4. Compute the within-cluster sum of squared errors (SSE) of the final partition. (6)
