Question 1 · Genetic algorithms 25 marks

(a) 15 marks

A GA solves a six-city travelling salesman problem. A chromosome is the order in which the cities are visited, and the tour returns from the last city to the first. The distances are:

123456
1047695
2403867
3730548
4685036
5964305
6578650

The fitness of a tour of length \(L\) is \(f = 100/L\). Two parents are \[ P_1 = 1\ 3\ 5\ 2\ 6\ 4, \qquad P_2 = 2\ 4\ 1\ 6\ 3\ 5. \]

  1. Calculate the tour length of each parent. (4)
  2. Apply partially mapped crossover (PMX), copying the segment at positions 3–5 from \(P_1\). Show every mapping step. (5)
  3. Calculate the tour length of the child, and the fitness of both parents and the child. (3)
  4. Explain why every crossover and mutation operator for this problem must return a permutation, and name the mutation operator that changes the fewest edges of a tour. (3)

(b) 10 marks

Apply cycle crossover to the parents \[ A = 1\ 2\ 3\ 4\ 5\ 6\ 7\ 8, \qquad B = 8\ 4\ 2\ 3\ 7\ 5\ 6\ 1. \]

  1. Identify all the cycles, as sets of positions. (4)
  2. Give child 1, taking the cycles alternately from \(A\), \(B\), \(A\), …, starting with the cycle that contains position 1. (3)
  3. Give child 2 (the complementary child). (2)
  4. State the property that cycle crossover guarantees for every gene. (1)

Question 3 · SVM and Fisher LDA 25 marks

(a) 13 marks

A soft-margin linear SVM with \(C = 2\) is trained. The samples, labels and optimal Lagrange multipliers are:

\(i\)\(\mathbf x_i\)\(d_i\)\(\alpha_i\)
1(1, 0)+10.125
2(2, 2)+11.375
3(0, 0)−11.625
4(1, 2)−11.875
5(0.5, 0)+12
6(3, 1)+10
  1. Verify the equality constraint of the dual problem. (1)
  2. Calculate the optimal weight vector \(\mathbf w\). (3)
  3. State which samples should be used to calculate the bias \(b\), and why. (2)
  4. Calculate \(b\). (2)
  5. Calculate the width of the margin. (1)
  6. Calculate the slack variable \(\xi_5\) and state where sample 5 lies. (2)
  7. Classify \(\mathbf x=(1.2,\ 0.9)\), give its distance from the decision boundary, and comment on the confidence. (2)

(b) 12 marks

A two-class problem has

\[ \mathbf m_1=\begin{bmatrix}5\\3\end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}1\\1\end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf S_1=\begin{bmatrix}20&4\\4&12\end{bmatrix},\quad \mathbf S_2=\begin{bmatrix}16&2\\2&20\end{bmatrix}. \]
  1. Write down the criterion that Fisher’s linear discriminant maximises, define every matrix in it, and explain what maximising it achieves. (3)
  2. Calculate \(\mathbf S_W\) and the Fisher weight vector \(\mathbf w\). (4)
  3. Calculate \(w_0\) so that the boundary passes midway between the projected means. (2)
  4. Classify \(\mathbf x=(3,\ 2.2)\). (1)
  5. Calculate the distance of \(\mathbf x\) from the boundary and judge the confidence of the decision against a suitable reference. (2)
