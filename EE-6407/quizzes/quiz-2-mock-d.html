Q1. Classifier evaluation (40 marks)

A spam filter is tested on 300 emails: 100 are spam (positive) and 200 are not. The filter misses 20 of the spam emails and wrongly flags 20 of the genuine ones.

  1. Construct the confusion matrix, with rows = predicted class and columns = actual class. Label TP, FP, FN and TN. (8)
  2. Compute the accuracy, error rate, sensitivity (recall), specificity, precision and F-score. Show the formula and substitution for each. (24)
  3. The filter is then run on a mailbox with twice as many genuine emails (400) and the same 100 spam. It behaves the same within each class. Which of your six metrics stay the same and which change? Give the new precision and F-score, and explain the difference in one sentence. (8)
Your answer: Q1
