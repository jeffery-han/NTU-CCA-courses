Question 1 · RRP robot (12 marks)

A horizontal two-link arm has two revolute joints and a vertical prismatic carriage. Joint 1 turns about the upward vertical axis at the base. Joint 2 turns about a parallel upward axis at the elbow. Link 1 has length \(L_1=3\); link 2 has length \(L_2=2\). The arm lies at fixed height \(h=4\) when the carriage displacement is zero. Joint 3 translates the tool point upward by the signed distance \(q_3\) along a vertical rail at the end of link 2. All lengths use the same units. The tool point lies on that rail; there is no extra tool offset or wrist joint.

  1. (a) 3 marks. Draw frames \(\{0\},\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\), showing the origin, \(x_k\), and \(z_k\) of each. Place frame 1 at the elbow, frame 2 at the vertical rail, and frame 3 at the tool point.
  2. (b) 3 marks. Fill the D–H table \((\theta_k,d_k,a_k,\alpha_k)\). Mark the variable in each of the three rows. State where the fixed height \(h\) enters.
  3. (c) 3 marks. Write \({}^{0}T_1\), \({}^{1}T_2\), and \({}^{2}T_3\).
  4. (d) 3 marks. Multiply them to obtain \({}^{0}T_3\) in terms of \(\theta_1,\theta_2,q_3\). Then evaluate the tool point and rotation matrix for \(\theta_1=90^\circ,\theta_2=-90^\circ,q_3=2\). Give one physical check of your answer.

Question 2 · Pick and place (8 marks)

Figure 2 is a top view. Both parts are rectangular blocks on a horizontal table, and \(z_0\) points upward. A parallel-jaw gripper picks A from above, contacting its two long side faces. It then places A on B so their centroids and long axes align. The gripper keeps holding A during the move. A's long axis is initially along \(x_0\); B's is along \(y_0\).

PartCurrent centroid \((x_0,y_0,z_0)\)FootprintHeight
A\((4,9,1)\)6 along \(x_0\), 2 along \(y_0\)2
B\((11,4,1.5)\)7 along \(y_0\), 3 along \(x_0\)3

Use \(R=[\,r_1\ r_2\ r_3\,]\), where \(r_1\) is tool normal, \(r_2\) is jaw sliding/open-close, and \(r_3\) is approach. For a top-down grasp take \(r_3=-z_0\). Either sign of the horizontal jaw axis is acceptable if your frame is right-handed and your pick/place orientations are physically consistent.

  1. (a) 3 marks. Give \(r_2\) and \(r_1\) at the pick pose, with one sentence explaining why \(r_2\) is perpendicular to A's long axis. Write \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{pick}}\).
  2. (b) 3 marks. Find A's destination centroid height when it rests on B. Rotate A to align its long axis with B's. Write one valid \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{place}}\).
  3. (c) 2 marks. Check both rotation matrices are right-handed and state how far the object turns about \(z_0\) between pick and place.

Section C · Optional prediction (5 marks, untimed)

This section probes in-scope manipulator tools beyond the two recalled question types. Use your own valid pick matrix from Question 2.

  1. (a) 3 marks. Write the general inverse of a rigid transform \(T=\begin{bmatrix}R&p\\0&1\end{bmatrix}\), then give \(({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{pick}})^{-1}\).
  2. (b) 2 marks. For tool roll angle \(q_n=0\), calculate the course's tool configuration vector \(w=[\,p^{\mathsf T},\ e^{q_n/\pi}r_3^{\mathsf T}\,]^{\mathsf T}\) at pick.
