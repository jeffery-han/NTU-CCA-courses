Question 1 · RRP arm (12 marks)

A two-link horizontal arm has a vertical lifting rail at its tip. Joint 1 rotates about the upward vertical base axis. Joint 2 rotates about a parallel upward vertical axis at the elbow. Joint 3 moves the tool point upward along the vertical rail by signed displacement \(q_3\). At \(q_3=0\), the horizontal links and tool point are at height \(h\) above the floor. There is no extra tool offset or wrist joint.

\(L_1\)\(L_2\)\(h\)\(\theta_1\)\(\theta_2\)\(q_3\)
435\(90^\circ\)\(90^\circ\)1
  1. (a) 3 marks. Draw frames \(\{0\},\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\) and mark each origin, \(z_k\), and \(x_k\). Put frame 1 at the elbow, frame 2 at the rail, and frame 3 at the tool point.
  2. (b) 3 marks. Write the three D–H rows \((\theta_k,d_k,a_k,\alpha_k)\); mark each joint variable and show where \(h\) enters.
  3. (c) 3 marks. Write \({}^{0}T_1\), \({}^{1}T_2\), and \({}^{2}T_3\), including each complete translation column.
  4. (d) 3 marks. Give symbolic \({}^{0}T_3\), then evaluate its rotation and position at the values in the table. Check your result against the link directions in a quick top-view sketch of the numeric pose.

Question 2 · Pick and place (8 marks)

Two rectangular blocks rest flat on a table. The gripper picks A from above by contacting its two long side faces, keeps holding it, and places it on B with their centroids and long axes aligned. The base axes \(x_0,y_0\) lie in the table plane; \(z_0\) points upward. All coordinates and dimensions use the same units.

PartCurrent centroid \((x_0,y_0,z_0)\)FootprintHeight
A\((5,11,1)\)8 along \(y_0\); 2 along \(x_0\)2
B\((12,4,1.5)\)6 along \(x_0\); 3 along \(y_0\)3

Use \(R=[\,r_1\ r_2\ r_3\,]\): normal, jaw sliding/open-close, approach. For top-down contact, take \(r_3=-z_0\). Either consistent sign of the jaw axis is acceptable.

  1. (a) 3 marks. Draw two jaws around A's long faces. State the jaw closing vector \(r_2\), calculate \(r_1\), and give \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{pick}}\).
  2. (b) 3 marks. Find A's destination centroid and give a physically consistent \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{place}}\) that aligns its long axis with B's.
  3. (c) 2 marks. Check both rotation matrices are right-handed and give the magnitude of the required turn about \(z_0\).
