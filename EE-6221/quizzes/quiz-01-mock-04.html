Question 1 · Articulated RRR arm (12 marks)

This arm has three revolute joints. Joint 1 turns a vertical waist axis \(z_0\). Joint 2 is a shoulder axis: horizontal and intersecting the waist axis at height \(h\). Joint 3 is an elbow axis: horizontal and parallel to the shoulder axis. Link 2 runs a distance \(L_2\) from shoulder to elbow; link 3 runs a distance \(L_3\) from elbow to the tool point. There is no separate wrist or tool offset.

Use \(x_0\) as a fixed horizontal reference. At \(\theta_1=0\), the arm's side plane is the \(x_0z_0\) plane and the shoulder/elbow axes point along \(-y_0\), into the side-view page. Choose positive \(\theta_2\) to raise link 2 from \(+x_0\) toward \(+z_0\), and positive \(\theta_3\) to raise link 3 relative to link 2. This fixes the sign convention for one valid standard D–H assignment.

\(h\)\(L_2\)\(L_3\)\(\theta_1\)\(\theta_2\)\(\theta_3\)

  1. (a) 3 marks. Draw frames \(\{0\},\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\). Show the vertical waist joint axis, the two parallel horizontal joint axes, and the origin of each frame (base, shoulder, elbow, tool point).
  2. (b) 3 marks. Write all three D–H rows \((\theta_k,d_k,a_k,\alpha_k)\). Mark the three joint variables. State which row has the \(90^\circ\) twist and why.
  3. (c) 3 marks. Write \({}^{0}T_1\), \({}^{1}T_2\), and \({}^{2}T_3\), including their complete translation columns.
  4. (d) 3 marks. Give symbolic \({}^{0}T_3\) and evaluate its rotation and position at the values above. Check the numeric position by tracing the two links in the \(x_0z_0\) side plane.

Question 2 · Pick and place (8 marks)

Two rectangular blocks rest flat on a table. The gripper picks A from above by contacting its two long side faces, keeps holding it, and places it on B with their centroids and long axes aligned. The base axes \(x_0,y_0\) lie in the table plane; \(z_0\) points upward. All coordinates and dimensions use the same units.

PartCurrent centroid \((x_0,y_0,z_0)\)FootprintHeight
A\((5,11,1)\)8 along \(y_0\); 2 along \(x_0\)2
B\((12,4,1.5)\)6 along \(x_0\); 3 along \(y_0\)3

Use \(R=[\,r_1\ r_2\ r_3\,]\): normal, jaw sliding/open-close, approach. For top-down contact, take \(r_3=-z_0\). Either consistent sign of the jaw axis is acceptable.

  1. (a) 3 marks. Draw two jaws around A's long faces. State the jaw closing vector \(r_2\), calculate \(r_1\), and give \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{pick}}\).
  2. (b) 3 marks. Find A's destination centroid and give a physically consistent \({}^{0}T_{\mathrm{place}}\) that aligns its long axis with B's.
  3. (c) 2 marks. Check both rotation matrices are right-handed and give the magnitude of the required turn about \(z_0\).
